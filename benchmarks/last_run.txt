cache-eviction-lru-lfu benchmarks - python 3.11.9
This file measures hit rate, memory, update cost and correctness of
five eviction policies against the exact-optimal MIN baseline.

trace time-shift, ops 30000, C=120; cells are hit rate
decay        I=1000    I=10000   I=100000 sweeps@I1
1.00         0.3161     0.3161     0.3161        0

LRU on the same trace: 0.7804    MIN: 0.8156
decay=1.00 is flat across intervals (0.3161, 0.3161, 0.3161), sweeps=0:
with no decay the sweep is skipped: that row has no knob left

Interpretation: the knob alone moves LFU by 0.4721 hit rate: best is decay
  0.00 at interval 1000 (0.7883, 30 sweeps), worst is decay 1.00 at interval
  1000 (0.3161, 0 sweeps, 3 interval cells tie at that rate). Against LRU at
  0.7804 the best setting gains +0.0078 and the worst loses -0.4643, and MIN
  sits at 0.8156, so "LFU" here is a family whose endpoints differ more than
  the distance from LRU to its own best alternative. The flat row is the
  reason: leaving aging off entirely sits 0.4643 below LRU on this trace.

Section 7. Update cost per operation (timing)
==============================================================================
ops 20000, C=400, 2 warm-ups discarded then best-of-5.
Construction, including MIN's trace index, is outside the timed region.
policy    best us/op  last us/op   spread us    vs FIFO
fifo          1.1173      1.1897      0.0724       1.00
lru           1.3370      1.4858      0.1488       1.20
two_q         1.7887      1.8382      0.0494       1.60
lfu           2.3809      2.5533      0.1724       2.13
belady        1.9059      2.2013      0.2954       1.71
noise floor (largest pass-to-pass spread of an identical batch): 0.2954 us/op
anything inside that is a tie; this machine is running other jobs.

Interpretation: best-of-5 per operation: lfu 2.3809 us/op, fifo 1.1173 us/op,
  ratio 2.13. 3 of the 4 non-FIFO policies separate from FIFO by more than the
  0.2954 us/op floor, and 2 of the 10 policy pairs are inside the floor and
  therefore ties. LFU's extra 1.2635 us/op over FIFO is the cost of the bucket
  bookkeeping, not of a different asymptotic class.
